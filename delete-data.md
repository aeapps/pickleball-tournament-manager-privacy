Pickleball Tournament Manager: Request Data Deletion

  Last Updated: September 23, 2026

  This page is for Pickleball Tournament Manager (called Pickleball Tournament Runner on the App Store), published by
  Accessible Ethical Apps LLC. The app has no accounts and no sign-up, so there is no account to delete. The only data
  that can exist about you outside your own device is described below, and all of it can be deleted.

  What can be stored on our server

  1. The shared copy of a tournament. A tournament is uploaded to our server only when its organizer turns on "Share
  with a join code" for it and saves. The shared copy contains the tournament name, the team and player names as the
  organizer typed them, the scores, the standings, and the tournament settings. It is identified by its 4-character
  join code.

  2. Feedback you chose to send. If you used the feedback form on the More tab, the message you wrote and the email
  address you optionally provided are stored.

  Nothing else is collected. Tournaments that were never shared exist only on the device that created them.

  Delete it yourself, right away

  A shared tournament: on the device that shared it, open the tournament, tap Edit, turn off "Share with a join code"
  and save, or delete the tournament in the app. The shared copy is removed from the server immediately (or the next
  time that device is online). Shared copies also expire on their own 90 days after they were last changed.

  Everything else about your tournaments lives only on your device and is removed when you delete the tournament in
  the app or uninstall the app.

  Ask us to delete it

  If you cannot do the above (for example the sharing device is no longer available, you were named in someone else's
  tournament, or you want your feedback removed), send a request to:

  [accessible.ethical@proton.me](mailto:accessible.ethical@proton.me?subject=Pickleball%20Tournament%20Manager%20data%20deletion%20request)

  Please include whichever of these applies, so we can find the data:

  - the join code of the shared tournament (or the tournament name and roughly when it was shared), and, if you were a
  player in someone else's tournament, the name to remove;
  - the email address you entered in the feedback form, if you gave one, or the approximate date and wording of your
  feedback.

  No account, password, or other identification is needed. You may also ask which data we hold before deciding.

  What happens next

  We delete the shared copy and/or your feedback within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner, and confirm by
  email if you gave us an address to reply to. Once deleted, the data is gone from our server and from our service
  provider (Google Firebase); we keep no other records, backups, or copies of it. Nothing about you is retained after
  deletion because the app collects nothing else.

  The full privacy policy is at https://aeapps.github.io/pickleball-tournament-manager-privacy/
