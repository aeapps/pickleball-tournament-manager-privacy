Pickleball Tournament Manager Privacy Policy

  Last Updated: July 18, 2026

  Overview

  Pickleball Tournament Manager is a mobile application for organizing pickleball tournaments. This policy explains what
  information the app handles and how.

  Information We Collect

  The app is designed to keep your data private.

  Tournament data (tournaments, teams, players, scores, and standings) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to any
  server.

  Feedback you choose to send. The app includes an optional in-app feedback form on the About screen. If, and only if, you
  choose to use it, the feedback message you write and the email address you optionally provide are sent to the developer. You
  are never required to submit feedback or provide an email to use the app.

  We do not collect location data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or any personal information beyond what you
  voluntarily submit through the feedback form.

  Data Storage

  Tournament data remains exclusively on your device. Feedback submissions (your message and, if provided, your email) are
  stored using Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, a hosting service, on the developer's behalf. They are accessible only to
  the developer.

  Third-Party Services

  The app uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) solely to store feedback you submit. Google processes this data as our
  service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The app does not integrate with any
  advertising networks, analytics services, social media platforms, or data brokers.

  How We Use Your Information

  Feedback and any email you provide are used only to read your feedback, respond to you, and improve the app. They are never
  used for advertising or tracking.

  Data Sharing

  We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Feedback data is not shared with anyone other than the service provider
  (Google Firebase) used to store it.

  Data Retention and Deletion

  You may request that your submitted feedback and email be deleted at any time by contacting us at the email below.

  Children's Privacy

  The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The feedback form and email field are
  entirely optional and user-provided.

  Changes to This Policy

  Updates will be noted by changing the Last Updated date at the top of this document.

  Contact

  Questions or deletion requests can be directed to: accessible.ethical@proton.me
