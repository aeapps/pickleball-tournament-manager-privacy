Privacy Policy for Pickleball Tournament Manager
Last Updated: March 4, 2026
Overview
Pickleball Tournament Manager is a mobile application for organizing pickleball tournaments. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how we handle your information.
Information Collection
Pickleball Tournament Manager does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data you enter into the app — including tournament details, team names, player names, scores, and results — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage.
We do not collect:

Personal identification information
Email addresses or contact information
Location data
Usage analytics or behavioral data
Device identifiers or advertising IDs

Data Storage
All tournament data is stored exclusively on your device. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party. If you uninstall the app or clear the app's data through your device settings, all stored tournament data will be permanently deleted.
Third-Party Services
Pickleball Tournament Manager does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, social media platforms, or data brokers. The app does not contain ads.
Children's Privacy
Pickleball Tournament Manager does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because all data remains on your device and is never transmitted, the app is suitable for use by individuals of all ages.
Data Sharing
We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. There is no data to share, sell, or transfer.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
